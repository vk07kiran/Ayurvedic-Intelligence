Yoga Asanas Guide

Adho Mukha Shvanasana (downward [facing] dog)

Steps:

Start on hands/knees.
Tuck toes, lift hips upward.
Press heels down, lengthen spine.
Hold, then release to knees.

Benefits:

  • Back Pain
  • Headaches/Migraines
  • Stress, Anxiety

Ardha Padmasana (half lotus)

Steps:

Sit with legs extended.
Bend right knee, place foot on left thigh.
Rest hands on knees.
Keep spine straight, breathe.

Benefits:

  • Opens Hips
  • Stretches Knees/Ankles
  • Calms Mind

Baddha Konasana (bound angle)

Steps:

Sit with legs extended.
Bend knees, bring soles together.
Hold feet, gently press knees down.
Lengthen spine, breathe.

Benefits:

  • Opens Hips/Groin
  • Stretches Inner Thighs
  • Relieves Menstrual Discomfort

Dvi Pada Kaundinyasana (two-legged Kaundinya)

Steps:

Start in Plank.
Shift weight, bring knees toward arm.
Balance on hands, extend legs.
Engage core, hold.

Benefits:

  • Strengthens Arms/Wrists
  • Tones Core
  • Improves Balance

Eka Pada Shirshasana (one-legged headstand)

Steps:

From Adho Mukha Shvanasana.
Bring one foot out to side.
Thread leg behind head.
Balance on head, extend free leg.

Benefits:

  • Strengthens Core/Arms
  • Improves Balance
  • Increases Focus
